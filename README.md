# Ship It?

A 10 to 12 minute browser game about launch review for an AI feature.

An AI feature is ready to launch, and you're on the review. Product wants a date; legal wants answers. Work out which rules apply, what has to change before launch, and what can wait. Partway through, the law changes.

**Play it:** https://jckesler.github.io/ship-it-ai-review/

## What it covers

- The EU AI Act's risk tiers, providers and deployers, and which obligations apply now versus later
- GDPR and AI: training on customer data, automated decisions (Article 22)
- US rules: California's automated decision-making rules, opt-outs and Global Privacy Control, Colorado's AI law
- HIPAA and AI vendors: business associate agreements, de-identification, recording consent
- Why AI compliance means watching for change

Each play draws one of three invented companies (a software vendor, a retailer and a health system), each with its own AI feature and its own news flash. The debrief can be saved as a PDF.

**The law is stated as of November 2026.** AI regulation is changing quickly, and this is an educational game, not legal advice.

## Running it

It's a single self-contained HTML file. Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or host it anywhere that serves static files. No build step, no server, no data collected.
